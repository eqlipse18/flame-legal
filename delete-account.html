Delete Your Account & Data

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your Flame account and the personal data associated with it. Flame is operated by Deepak Sahani.

Option 1 — Delete from within the app

  1. Open the Flame app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings → Account & Security.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the request. Your account and profile will be removed.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can't access the app, email us from the address linked to your Flame account (including the Google account you used to sign in, if you signed in with Google) at inflameapp.support@gmail.com with the subject line "Delete My Account". We will process your request within 30 days.

What data is deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

  • Your profile information (name, date of birth, gender, bio, hometown, and preferences)
  • Your photos
  • Your matches, likes, and messages
  • Your location data
  • Your account and login identifiers, and your push-notification token

What data may be retained

We may retain a limited amount of data for a short period where required for legal, security, fraud- prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes. This data is kept only as long as necessary and is then deleted. Records of subscription transactions processed by Google Play are retained as required by law.

Retention period: account and personal data are deleted within 30 days of a valid request, except for the limited categories noted above.
Subscriptions: deleting your Flame account does not cancel an active Google Play subscription. Please cancel it in the Google Play Store app under Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions to avoid further charges.

Contact

Questions about deleting your account or data? Contact us at inflameapp.support@gmail.com.
