Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Flame is committed to preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM) on our platform. We have a zero-tolerance policy toward any content or conduct that sexualizes, endangers, or exploits minors. This page describes the standards and measures we maintain to keep our service safe.

Adults only: Flame is strictly for people aged 18 and older. Anyone found to be under 18, or misrepresenting their age, is removed from the platform.

1. Zero-Tolerance Policy

We strictly prohibit, and act against, any of the following:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form.
  • Grooming, solicitation, or sexualization of minors.
  • Use of the app by anyone under the age of 18.
  • Any content or behavior that endangers or exploits children.

2. Preventing Underage Use

  • Users must confirm they are 18 or older during registration and provide a date of birth.
  • Our Google Play listing is restricted to adults, and we enable Google Play's setting to block users determined to be minors.
  • Accounts found to belong to minors are terminated.

3. In-App Reporting

Every user can report another user or content directly within the app. Reports that involve potential child safety concerns are prioritized. Users can also block any other user at any time. Additionally, anyone can report a concern by emailing inflameapp.support@gmail.com.

4. Response and Enforcement

  • We review reports of child safety violations and remove violating content and accounts.
  • We take action against users who violate these standards, including permanent bans.
  • Where CSAM is identified, we act to remove it and preserve relevant information as required by law.

5. Reporting to Authorities

We comply with all applicable child safety laws, including India's Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012 and the Information Technology Act, 2000. Where required, we report apparent instances of child sexual abuse material and exploitation to the relevant authorities, including through India's National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in), and to recognized bodies such as the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) where applicable.

6. Designated Point of Contact

Our designated point of contact for child safety and CSAE matters is Deepak Sahani, reachable at inflameapp.support@gmail.com. This contact is able to respond to questions about our CSAM prevention practices and compliance.
