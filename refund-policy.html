Refund Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Flame Plus and Flame Ultra subscriptions are sold and billed by Google Play. This policy explains how to request a refund and how we handle requests. It does not limit any rights you have under the consumer protection laws of your country.

1. Requesting a refund through Google Play

Google Play offers its own refund process, which is usually the fastest option, especially soon after a purchase. To request a refund:

  1. Go to play.google.com → Order history (or the Play Store app → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history).
  2. Find your Flame purchase and select Request a refund or Report a problem.
  3. Follow the steps. Google decides these requests under the Google Play refund policies.

2. Requesting a refund from us

If you can't get a refund through Google Play, email us at inflameapp.support@gmail.com from the email linked to your Flame account, with:

  • The Google Play order number (it starts with GPA. and is in your purchase receipt email)
  • The email you use to sign in to Flame
  • A short description of the problem

We review each request individually and usually reply within 5 working days. We generally approve a refund when:

  • You were charged more than once for the same subscription period;
  • A premium feature you paid for did not work and we could not fix it within a reasonable time; or
  • You were charged after cancelling correctly before the renewal date.

Approved refunds are issued through Google Play to your original payment method. How long the money takes to appear depends on Google and your bank.

3. Cancelling vs. refunds

Cancelling a subscription stops future renewals. It does not, on its own, refund the current period; you keep premium features until that period ends. Partial or pro-rated refunds for unused time are not normally given, except where required by law or covered in section 2. See Subscriptions & Pricing for how to cancel.

4. When a refund is issued

When a subscription is refunded, the premium features for that purchase are removed from your account.

5. Account removal for rule violations

If we remove an account for breaking our Terms of Service or Child Safety Standards, the subscription is not refunded, except where required by law. You can still cancel future renewals in Google Play.

Deleting your Flame account does not cancel or refund your subscription. Cancel it in Google Play first.

6. Contact

Refund questions: inflameapp.support@gmail.com. Complaints can also be sent to our Grievance Officer (see our Privacy Policy).
