Subscriptions & Pricing

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Flame is free to use. You can create a profile, swipe, match and chat without paying. Two optional subscriptions, Flame Plus and Flame Ultra, unlock extra features. This page explains what each plan includes, how billing works, and how to cancel.

What each plan includes

Flame Plus

  • See who liked you
  • See who viewed your profile
  • 5 Superlikes per day
  • 10 Rewinds per day
  • 1 message request per day
  • Advanced filters
  • Hide your last seen and online status
  • Chat with all your matches

Flame Ultra

  • Everything in Flame Plus
  • Unlimited Superlikes
  • Unlimited Rewinds
  • Unlimited message requests
  • Ultra badge on your profile

Free plan

Without a subscription you can create a profile, swipe, match, and chat with your first matches. Likes you receive are shown as blurred previews.

Longer plans cost less per month. Prices are set in your local currency by Google Play and may differ by country. The exact price, including any applicable taxes, is always shown in the app and on the Google Play payment screen before you confirm a purchase.

How billing works

  • All payments are processed by Google Play and charged to the payment method on your Google account. Flame does not see or store your card details.
  • Auto-renewal: your subscription renews automatically at the end of each billing period, for the same length and at the then-current price, unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends.
  • Price changes: if the price of your plan changes, Google Play will notify you before the new price applies, as required by Google Play's policies.
  • Changing plans: you can upgrade or downgrade from the Premium screen in the app. Google Play handles any proration or timing of the change.

How to cancel

  1. Open the Google Play Store app.
  2. Tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  3. Select Flame and tap Cancel subscription.

You can also open this page from Settings → Manage subscription in the Flame app.

When you cancel, you keep premium features until the end of the period you already paid for. After that, your account returns to the free plan; your profile, matches and chats are not deleted.

Deleting your Flame account does not cancel your subscription. Please cancel in Google Play first to avoid further charges.

Restoring a purchase

If you reinstall Flame or switch phones, sign in with the same Flame account and the same Google account, then tap Restore purchases on the Premium screen.
