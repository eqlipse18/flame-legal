Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Flame ("Flame", "we", "us", or "our"), operated by Deepak Sahani, collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Flame mobile application (the "App"). By creating an account or using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

Age requirement: Flame is a dating application intended only for people aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly allow anyone under 18 to use the App.

1. Information We Collect

To provide a dating service, we collect the following categories of information:

  • Account information: your email address and authentication identifiers, managed through Amazon Cognito, used to create and secure your account.
  • Information from Google Sign-In: if you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile picture from your Google account. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Profile information: your name, date of birth / age, gender, who you are interested in, relationship goals, bio, hometown, and other details you choose to add to your profile. Some of this is considered sensitive personal information.
  • Photos: the profile photos you upload. When you add a photo, the App uses automated face detection (Google ML Kit, which runs on your device) to check that the photo shows a face. This check only detects whether a face is present; it does not identify you, and it does not create or store a facial recognition profile.
  • Precise location: your device's location, used to show nearby people and distance. You can control this through your device permissions.
  • Messages and interactions: the text messages, reactions, likes, and matches you send and receive within the App.
  • Device and usage data: device identifiers, a push-notification token (via Firebase Cloud Messaging), app usage, and diagnostic/crash data (via Sentry) to keep the App stable.
  • Purchase information: records of subscriptions you buy. Payments are processed by Google Play; we do not receive or store your full payment card details.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create your account, build your profile, and operate core features (feed, matching, chat).
  • To show you relevant people based on your preferences and location.
  • To provide subscriptions and premium features, and to verify purchases with Google Play.
  • To keep the App safe — including block/report handling, fraud prevention, and moderation.
  • To send notifications you have enabled.
  • To monitor performance, fix crashes, and improve the App.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. How We Share Your Information

  • With other users: your profile details and photos are visible to other users as part of the dating experience. Messages are shared with the users you chat with.
  • With service providers: we use trusted third parties to run the App, including Amazon Web Services (authentication, database, storage, hosting), Google (Sign-In, Firebase push notifications, and Google Play billing), and Sentry (crash and error reporting). They process data only to provide these services to us.
  • For legal reasons: to comply with law, respond to lawful requests from authorities, enforce our Terms, or protect the rights and safety of users and the public.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Data Retention

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete or anonymize your personal information within 30 days, except where we must retain certain data for legal, safety, or fraud-prevention purposes.

5. Deleting Your Account and Data

You can delete your account at any time from within the App (Profile → Settings → Account & Security → Delete Account), or by emailing us at inflameapp.support@gmail.com. See our Account Deletion page for details on what is deleted and what may be retained.

6. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections, encrypted authentication tokens, and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information, withdraw consent, or object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us at inflameapp.support@gmail.com.

8. Children's Privacy

Flame is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have collected such information, we will delete it and remove the account. See our Child Safety Standards.

9. Where Your Data Is Stored

Your information is stored on Amazon Web Services infrastructure in the Mumbai (India) region. Some service providers, such as Google and Sentry, may process limited data in other countries. We take steps to ensure your information is handled in line with this Policy wherever it is processed.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you within the App.
